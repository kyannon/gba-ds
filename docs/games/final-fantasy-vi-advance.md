What GBA DS does with this game. Each rule is a promise the profile keeps; the regression tests check them (the cases citing a rule are listed by `tools/regress.py --list final-fantasy-vi-advance`). Change a rule here first when the behaviour is meant to change, then the profile, then the tests and goldens. Rule numbers are never reused: a dropped rule stays, marked *retired*.

Every game also follows the [general rules](README.md#general-rules) (GEN-1 to GEN-11).

## Why these screens

Asked what the game would have put on a second GBA screen: the one screen a player keeps opening while walking about is START's menu, which shows the whole party at once (portrait, name, class, level, HP and MP), where the party is, and the play time, steps and gil. The game has no map screen of its own: on the world map it draws a small map in a corner of the game screen, which SELECT shows or hides. That small map is the one thing worth a second screen on the world map (the owner's choice, Nov 18, 2026), so it moves over, twice as big (FF6-10, an exception to the first principle, as the Legacy of Goku games' world maps are). Everywhere else the second screen follows the Dawn of Souls games ([final-fantasy-1-2-dawn-of-souls.md](final-fantasy-1-2-dawn-of-souls.md)): START's menu without its command list, taken from a hidden copy, kept as the party screen through battles, and the logo when nothing is going on. (The owner's choices there: the party shown in a battle as it was before it, and the logo once the party has been wiped out.)

## Rules

**FF6-1. Logo.** The logo is the title's FINAL FANTASY VI lettering with the ADVANCE bar under it (BG0), without PRESS ANY BUTTON, the New Game / Load Game / Extra list or its hand, or the copyright line.

**FF6-2. Outside the game.** On the title, the Load Game and Extra screens (Bestiary, Music Player) and before them, the second screen shows the logo, any pictures from a game played before are dropped, and no captures are tried. Told by the party roster not being set up: the game keeps its characters in 16 records of 37 bytes from 0x02001600 (the first byte of each the character's number, from 0; the name from the third byte, 6 bytes, in the game's own letter codes; the level at +8, HP at +9, maximum HP at +0xB, MP at +0xD, maximum MP at +0xF; the same in all three releases), set up only in a game: outside one the first byte is 0xFF or 0 and the level 0. In a game: the first record's character number is 0 and its level above 0.

**FF6-3. A game just started.** On the first scenes of a new game, before any picture has been taken, the second screen keeps the logo (nothing opens there: the hidden copy's menu never comes up).

**FF6-4. Walking about: the party screen.** Walking about anywhere but the world map (FF6-10: towns, dungeons, the Veldt), the second screen shows START's menu on the hidden copy, as the party screen: the party's portraits, names, classes, levels, HP and MP, the place name, and the time, steps and gil, without the command list (Items, Abilities, Equip... Save) and without the hand. The command window's frame stays, empty, where the list was; the game draws the list as text on the same layer as the party's, so the list's tiles are cleared on the hidden copy (the live game is never touched). It's taken again at every refresh, and at once on entering another map. Told by START's menu being up: the display registers' values in its main screen (DISPCNT 0x1540 with BG0's control 0x0F49 in all three releases).

**FF6-5. A menu or screen open in the game.** With START's menu, one of its screens (Items, Abilities, Equip, Relics, Status, Formation, Config) or the Save screen open in the game, the second screen shows what it would walking about (FF6-4, FF6-10): the hidden copy closes them first (B, as many times as it takes for START to bring the menu up, at most five times).

**FF6-6. Last picture stays.** In a conversation, a scene the story plays, a shop, an inn, the fade between maps, and anywhere else the menu (or on the world map, the minimap) doesn't come up on the hidden copy, the last picture stays; before the first, the logo.

**FF6-7. Battles.** In a battle (on the world map too), from its first frame to the end of its results, the second screen shows the party kept from walking about (FF6-4): its HP, MP and play time are from before the battle and don't change during it. When no picture of this party has been kept (a state loaded straight into a battle, another file's party), the last picture stays: the logo if there's none yet. After a battle, the party screen (or on the world map, the minimap, FF6-10) is taken again at once. Told by the display registers a battle sets: DISPCNT 0x1600 with BG0's control 0x560C, BG1's 0x5408 and BG2's 0x5A08 (the same in all three releases; the registers go back to the field's when the results are done). (Until Nov 18, 2026 by the halfword at 0x02000002 being 0: it is 0xFFFF after a game is loaded, but stays 0 after the first battle, so after a battle the party was shown on the world map for good and the minimap never came back: a bug report from the device.) The kept party is shown only for the party it was taken of, told by the 16 records' character numbers and names and each character's party byte (0x02001850 + the character's number: bits 0-2 the party it belongs to), which a battle doesn't change.

**FF6-8. Game screen untouched.** Nothing leaves the game screen but the world map's corner minimap (FF6-11).

**FF6-9. Game over.** When the whole party falls in a battle (every character in it at 0 HP), the second screen shows the logo from that moment, and the picture kept is dropped, until the party screen is taken again after the game is loaded or restarted. Told in a battle (FF6-7) by the party's HP in the game's battle memory, four halfwords from 0x02003BF4 (the party's four places in order; the same in all three releases), with the places that hold a character told by their maximum HP, four halfwords from 0x02003C1C, being above 0: every such place at 0 HP. A party turned to stone, or out some other way that leaves HP above 0, isn't seen: the party kept stays until the game goes back to its title.

**FF6-10. The world map: the minimap, twice as big.** Walking about on the world map (the map number, the halfword at 0x02000082, 0 or 1: the World of Balance and the World of Ruin; the airship flies over the same maps), the second screen shows the game's own minimap twice as big, 128 by 128 pixels in the middle of the second screen on black: the game draws it in the game screen's bottom right corner as sprites (sixteen of 16 by 16 pixels, tiles 512 to 590 in palette 5, in a 64 by 64 square from 168, 92, and the red marker of where the party is, tile 768 in the same palette), and the hidden copy draws those alone (no layers, no other sprites), so it's the minimap as the game draws it, without the world beneath it that shows through on the game screen. It shows whether or not the player has switched the corner minimap off with SELECT (the game keeps that in bit 0 of the byte at 0x020011F6): on the hidden copy, SELECT is pressed once if it's off. It's taken again at every refresh, so the marker follows the party (the airship too), and at once on entering another map or leaving a battle. With START's menu or one of its screens open in the game, the hidden copy closes them first (B, as many times as it takes, at most five), as in FF6-5. Where the minimap doesn't come up on the hidden copy (a map numbered 0 or 1 that has none), the party screen is shown instead (FF6-4). The party is kept for battles all the same (FF6-7): while the party's on the world map, the hidden copy also takes START's menu without showing it, on the first capture and every fourth (the one the minimap was taken from, then B, then START). An exception to the Philosophy's first principle (the game's own size), as the Legacy of Goku games' world maps are: the minimap is small, and a map on the second screen is only useful when it can be read at a glance. *By hand: the World of Ruin and the airship (the cases play the World of Balance, on foot).*

**FF6-11. The corner minimap leaves the game screen.** The minimap's sprites (FF6-10's: the sixteen and the marker) aren't drawn on the game screen, wherever the game draws them, so the same map isn't on both screens (Philosophy 2). SELECT still does what it does in the game (the byte at 0x020011F6 changes) but it no longer shows on the game screen; the second screen shows the minimap whichever way it's set (FF6-10).

## Not covered yet

- **The minimap in the Europe and Japan releases**: the cases reach the world map only in the USA release (the owner's save); the Europe and Japan saves are in dungeons. The sprites and addresses are expected to be the same (the profile is shared); if the minimap doesn't come up there, the second screen shows the party screen and the corner minimap stays on the game screen.
- **The World of Ruin and the airship**: the cases play the World of Balance on foot (the owner's save; the Ruin and the airship's minimaps are expected to be the same sprites). Chocobos, the Falcon and the other vehicles: the same.
- **Characters in other parties**: where the story splits the party (the Narshe defence, the floating continent, the final dungeon), the party screen shows whichever party the menu shows; the game-over rule only looks at the characters in the battle.
- **The Bestiary and the Esper, Abilities and Equip screens in a battle's place**: not shown; the second screen keeps the party.
- **The Europe release's other languages (French, German, Spanish, Italian)**: the cases play it in English.

## How the states were made

All from the owner's GameShark saves (`tools/sharkport.py`: `saves/BZ6E-gogo-wob.sav` from GameFAQs, notes: "Start game in WoB near Narshe right after Kefka battle w/Gogo/Umaro in party"; `saves/BZ6P-kefkas-tower.sav`: file 1 in Kefka's Tower, four characters at level 99; `saves/BZ6J-narshe-mines.sav`: file 1 with Terra and two Imperial soldiers in the Narshe mines), loaded in the app (the harness has no battery save: Load Game, file 1, Yes) and played on in the harness. The title's states are from power-on (a press of A through each screen). The minimap's states (Nov 18, 2026): `us-wob-gogo.ss` (the corner minimap on), `us-wob-gogo-hidden.ss` (SELECT pressed once: the player's hidden it). Battles: LEFT and RIGHT (UP and DOWN in the dungeons) until the encounter, `us-wob-battle.ss`, `eu-kefka-tower-battle.ss`, `jp-narshe-mines-battle.ss`.
