What GBA DS does with this game. Each rule is a promise the profile keeps; the regression tests check them (the cases citing a rule are listed by `tools/regress.py --list mario-and-luigi-superstar-saga`). Change a rule here first when the behaviour is meant to change, then the profile, then the tests and goldens. Rule numbers are never reused: a dropped rule stays, marked *retired*.

Every game also follows the [general rules](README.md#general-rules) (GEN-1 to GEN-7).

## Rules

**ML-1. Logo.** On the title and in battles (ML-3, behind the battle's boxes and meters: ML-5), the second screen shows the logo: MARIO & LUIGI and SUPERSTAR SAGA (in Japan マリオ&ルイージRPG), without the sky, PRESS START, the suitcase or the copyright line: no sky blue at the lettering's edge, no specks of the background around its shadow, no stray dots in its corners.

**ML-2. Bros. Info, always.** Walking about, the second screen is the Bros. Info screen (SELECT's menu, its third icon), as the game draws it, filling the screen, once it has settled (its opening and the brothers' turn over) and only if it is Bros. Info (its frame checked; never the field or a menu): the page of the brother you're controlling, the one in front (START swaps them; the page follows within a moment). It's opened in the background on a silent copy of the game about once a second; your game is never paused. (Until v1.4.5's later test builds, Mario's and Luigi's pages in turn, 5 seconds each.)

**ML-3. Elsewhere.** In a battle, the second screen shows the logo, with the battle's boxes and meters over it (ML-5); Bros. Info again once you're walking about after it. In scenes, conversations and the game's own menus it keeps the last picture. Nothing else leaves the game screen.

**ML-4. One screen.** With one screen, the game plays as it is.

**ML-5. Battles: the boxes and meters on the second screen.** In a battle, from its first frame to the results:
- Mario's and Luigi's meters (HP and BP, bottom left on the game screen) go to the top left corner of the second screen, beside the top right box and above the item list's cursor.
- The information boxes at the top right (what the block does: "Use an Item", "Solo Attack") and the bottom right (the buttons) go to the same places on the second screen, with their text. So do the menus that open on the right side (the item list), with their cursor and icons.
- The box at the top left while you aim (the enemy's name) stays on the game screen, by the enemies, where the meters would be on the second screen.
- The command blocks (RUN, ITEM, SOLO...) and the A button prompt stay on the game screen, over the brother whose turn it is, and so does everything else: the brothers, the enemies, the attacks and their action command prompts (the big A or B a Bros. Attack shows by the brother who has to press it, and the "!" over a brother's head, too), and the damage numbers over an enemy or a brother.
- A conversation in a battle (the tutorials' speech balloons) stays on the game screen, by whoever is speaking, also while a box is up beside it on the second screen (the Starshade Bros.' Bros. Attack tutorial in Stardust Fields). (Until v1.5's test builds, a balloon shown while a box was up went to the second screen: issue #31.)
- The results (EXP, coins, items won) and any level-ups after them (the brother jumping as his level counts up, his name, the star, LEVEL UP! and the stat bonuses) stay on the game screen as the game draws them, with the logo alone on the second screen, until you're walking about again. (Until v1.4.12's test builds, a level-up's opening, between the results and LEVEL UP!, went to the second screen: issue #29.)

## Not covered yet

Battles (ML-5): tried in the first battles of the story (the Koopa Cruiser's Goombas, with its tutorial, and the Fighter Flies of Stardust Fields), in eleven battles at level 38 (rooms 0x076, 0x09A, 0x0C6, 0x0E6, 0x116, 0x15C, 0x163, 0x164, 0x16B, 0x18D, 0x194, entered by starting an endgame file in them: the brothers start on an enemy), and in sixteen boss battles at level 38 (see "Battles" below; named by the game's own name box: Bowser on the Koopa Cruiser, Queen Bean, Wiggler, Roy with the Time Bob-omb, Fawful's dome): choosing a block, the item list (the endgame's, scrolling), aiming at an enemy, the BROS block and its list (Splash, Chopper and Fire Bros., with the attack's level marks), attacks and dodging, damage numbers to four digits, three-digit meters, the results. A boss's message in its own box at the top (the Time Bob-omb's) stays on the game screen like a conversation, and the game hides the meters while it's up. Not tried: a Bros. Attack's own effects all the way through, the bosses' own results and what follows them (these came from an endgame file, the bosses already beaten), and battles at the story's own points.

Walking about (ML-2): every room from 0x000 to 0x1FF was tried in the USA release, started from an endgame save (see "Changing rooms" below): Bros. Info is taken in all 454 where you walk about, and in scenes and minigames (where the game takes no SELECT) the last picture stays. Only from the start of the story to the Koopa Cruiser and Stardust Fields was that by playing; the rooms after the first file came in from a save's file, not by walking in, so a room that only shows something special when you arrive the usual way hasn't been seen that way. Rooms above 0x1FF aren't tried. Europe and Japan were tried walking about on the Koopa Cruiser (the owner's states): their screen and lead bytes are at the USA's addresses, but Japan's menu cursor is 0x20 higher (until this was found, Japan's second screen kept the logo walking about); in battles, a Koopa Cruiser battle's blocks and results were tried in both (the owner's states, as in the USA), and Japan's Bowser fight on the device; their later battles aren't tried. One early report showed the field on the second screen, from before the report's 10 seconds: not reproduced; the frame check keeps anything but Bros. Info off.
