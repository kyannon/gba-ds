    GBA DS lays itself out for screens of this resolution, as on a handheld that has them. Changing it restarts the game where it is.

    Layout

    Follow screen rotation: upright, the screens stack; on its side, they sit side by side. GBA DS draws its screens at the detail chosen and your graphics card scales them to fit. On Two GBA screens a third draws every game pixel once, two thirds twice and the whole resolution three times, all exact; half and three quarters (1.5× and 2.25×) make some pixels wider than their neighbours.
